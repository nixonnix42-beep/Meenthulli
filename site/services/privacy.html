Meenthulli Home Services Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Meenthulli Home Services is run by Nixon, Sankarankovil, Tenkasi district, Tamil Nadu, India.

What we collect

When you place an order or booking, the app prepares a WhatsApp message with your name, phone number, address and the details you typed. This is sent only when you press Send in WhatsApp. We use it only to deliver your order or do your work, and to call you about it.

The app saves your name, phone, address and past orders on your own phone so you do not need to type them again. This data stays on your phone. We do not run our own server and we do not sell or share your data.
